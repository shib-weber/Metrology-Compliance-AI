## 📌 Executive Summary

**Metronox** is an enterprise AI-driven statutory metrology platform designed to automate physical packaging inspections across consumer goods (FMCG, Food, Cosmetics, and Pharmaceuticals). 

Combining client-side **real-time 1:1 contour tracking**, **adaptive CLAHE contrast equalization**, **multi-pass rotational OCR**, and **photogrammetric 3D digital twin synthesis**, Metronox detects compliance infractions (missing declarations, font deficits, improper MRP/USP representations) in under **1.8 seconds per SKU** and automatically issues court-admissible **Form V Violation Citations**.
